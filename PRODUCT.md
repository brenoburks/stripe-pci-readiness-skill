# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers, small merchants, technical advisers, and project stakeholders reviewing the PCI readiness of a Stripe payment integration. They need to understand scope, evidence, uncertainty, and remediation without reading source code or mistaking the report for certification.

## Product Purpose

Turn an evidence-led Stripe PCI readiness assessment into a portable report that is understandable on screen, printable to PDF, and safe to share with authorised stakeholders. Success means readers can find the outcome, distinguish facts from inference, trace material claims to primary sources, and identify the next action without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and accountable. The report should feel like a carefully prepared technical dossier, not a marketing page, automated scanner dashboard, legal certificate, or alarmist breach notice.

## Anti-references

- Generic cybersecurity dashboards with neon glows, radial scores, gauges, or fear-based imagery
- Decorative dark-mode hacker interfaces
- Compliance certificates, seals, or language that implies approval
- Dense exported spreadsheets with unreadable type and uncontrolled horizontal overflow
- AI-generated editorial styling that prioritises mood over evidence

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision-relevant outcome and its confidence.
2. Make evidence provenance visible at the point of use.
3. Treat uncertainty as information, not an empty state.
4. Separate executive comprehension from technical depth without hiding either.
5. Make the report trustworthy through restraint, consistency, and explicit limitations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the generated HTML. Preserve meaning without colour, support keyboard navigation and zoom, use semantic headings and tables, provide a skip link, maintain readable line lengths and touch targets, and produce legible print output. The report contains no required animation and no dependence on JavaScript.
